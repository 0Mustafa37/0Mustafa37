### 🛠️ What I do

At **Holouly Innovative Solutions** I own backend modules end to end on two live B2B platforms:

- **ETEPS**: field service management (ticketing, SLA, inventory, ERP integration)
- **Mdawm**: attendance, live tracking, violation monitoring, CRM integration

Day to day that means:

- Designing service layers with named domain exceptions and thin views
- Building bulk and idempotent APIs that handle partial failure cleanly (HTTP 207, per-item savepoints)
- Tuning PostgreSQL from execution plans: `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`, composite indexes, `work_mem`
- Maintaining ERP integrations (BAAN/Infor, SQL Server) and large data syncs with Celery
- Mentoring interns; I wrote our Django internship training plan and onboarding roadmap
